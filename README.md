APP NAME (max 30): PMPS Guards
SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80): Find security guard jobs or hire trained guards in Quetta.

PMPS by Pak Modern Protection Services (Pvt.) Ltd. connects security guards with jobs, and businesses with trained guards.
FOR GUARDS: register, upload your documents, browse jobs at hotels, malls, schools, hospitals, jewelry shops and restaurants, apply, and track your approval.
FOR BUSINESSES: send a guard request with business type, number of guards, shift and address.
Also: invite friends and earn points, plus clear Rules, Terms and Privacy Policy inside the app.
